Module: Risk and Knowledge Services
Process and techniques

This module provides three types of evidence: risk prediction, emergency knowledge, and route options. The LLM must not guess these results.

Risk model techniques
- Start with an explainable baseline and rule-based safety overrides.
- Split evaluation data by time and location to prevent data leakage.
- Focus on recall and false negatives for HIGH-risk cases.
- Calibrate probability scores.
- Use SHAP or reason codes for explanation, but do not describe them as proof of cause.
- Monitor model drift by location, season, and hazard type.

Disaster RAG techniques
- Use hybrid retrieval with BM25 and vector search, followed by reranking.
- Split documents by topic and procedure. Do not cut an emergency step in the middle.
- Filter by authority, geography, language, effective date, and expiry date.
- Return document_id, page, and section with each passage.
- Say that evidence was not found when retrieval confidence is too low.

Route techniques
- Calculate several route options and an exposure score for the full route.
- Treat official closures and evacuation routes as hard constraints.
- Treat delay and weather as weighted costs.
- Show time, distance, risk, and freshness for each option.
