Module: Web App
Main workflow

1. The user enters the origin, destination, travel date and time, preferences, and a question.
2. The app checks required fields, date format, and location data.
3. The app converts place names into coordinates when needed.
4. The user confirms the locations on a map.
5. The app sends a TravelRequest to the backend with an access token and request_id.
6. The app shows progress, such as checking weather, transport, and disaster alerts.
7. The app receives a JSON response or live stream from the backend.
8. The app shows the risk level, main action, reasons, routes, sources, and update time.
9. The app clearly displays one action: travel normally, change route, delay travel, or avoid travel.
10. For high risk, the app also shows emergency steps and official contact details.
11. A follow-up question uses the same conversation_id and enters the feedback loop.

Main outputs
- A validated TravelRequest
- A clear recommendation page
- Maps, risk details, and source links
- A feedback event for the next request
